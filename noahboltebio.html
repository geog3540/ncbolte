About Me

I am a student with strong interests in geographic information science, spatial analysis, and data visualization. My academic work focuses on understanding how spatial patterns emerge and how mapping techniques can reveal meaningful insights about demographic, environmental, and social processes. Professionally, I am interested in applying GIS tools to real‑world problems, especially those involving population data, urban systems, and environmental change. Outside of academics, I enjoy exploring new mapping tools, learning about wildlife and ecological systems, and working on creative projects that combine design and data.
